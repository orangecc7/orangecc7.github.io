Proposed Workshop at WSDM 2027

Management of Agentic Systems:
Databases, Graphs and the Future of Web Intelligence

Proposed for WSDM 2027February 19, 2027
Hong Kong

Proposal stage Acceptance, speakers and the final program are to be confirmed.

Introduction

Built on large language models and advanced through skill design, agent harnesses, and workflow engineering, agentic systems are redefining Web intelligence and transforming how we search, analyze, create, and conduct scientific research. However, their potential remains limited by weak grounding, inconsistent memory, brittle tool use, inefficient execution, opaque reasoning, and insufficient governance. Responding to this urgent need, this workshop discusses the evolution and management of agentic systems through the lenses of database research and graph data mining.

Database principles and techniques—including declarative interfaces, query and workflow optimization, transactional state management, provenance, access control, and auditability—can make agent workflows more reliable, scalable, and accountable. Graph techniques complement these foundations by strengthening agent memory, inter-agent communication, retrieval augmentation, and white-box reasoning, while supporting recursive self-improvement through iterative hypothesis generation, experimentation, and validation. To capture the intrinsic structure, hierarchy and shape, Riemannian geometry enables agents to recognize and reason about complex topological and relational patterns.

The workshop also critically examines the capability boundaries of agentic systems, from information retrieval to scientific research, with an emphasis on safety constraints and responsible governance. The program will feature invited talks, paper and demo presentations, panel discussions, and interactive breakout sessions. By bringing together researchers and practitioners from databases, graph mining, geometric learning, agentic AI, and trustworthy AI, we aim to establish a shared agenda for managing agentic systems and shaping the next generation of Web intelligence powered by reliable, capable, and well-governed agents.

Topics of Interest

The proposed workshop explores the following themes:

  • Database foundations for agentic systems: declarative interfaces, query and workflow optimization, transactional state management, and scalable execution.
  • Graph-grounded memory and retrieval: graph representations for memory, retrieval augmentation, provenance, and relational context.
  • Agent communication and coordination: inter-agent communication, tool use, orchestration, and dependable multi-agent workflows.
  • Geometric reasoning: Riemannian geometry and geometric learning for hierarchy, topology, and structured relations.
  • Recursive self-improvement: iterative hypothesis generation, experimentation, validation, and agent system improvement.
  • Capability boundaries and governance: evaluation, white-box reasoning, access control, auditability, safety constraints, and responsible deployment.

Call for Papers

The formal call will be published if the workshop is accepted.

We expect contributions that connect agent capabilities with reliable management and evaluation, including research on:

  • Agent workflow design, optimization and execution
  • Graph-based memory, retrieval and white-box reasoning
  • Geometric representations of relational or hierarchical structure
  • Evaluation, provenance, safety and governance of agentic systems

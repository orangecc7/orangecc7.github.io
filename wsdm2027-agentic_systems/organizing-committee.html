Workshop Organizers

Chen Jason Zhang

The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Google Scholar ↗

Chen (Jason) Zhang is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computing at PolyU. He received his Ph.D. from HKUST and previously served as a Research Assistant Professor at PolyU and Senior Manager at HKUST's Big Data Institute. His research spans artificial intelligence, data management, and human–AI collaboration, with applications in fintech, intelligent decision-making, scientific discovery, and AI-driven materials discovery. His work has appeared in VLDB, ICDE, AAAI, TKDE, and Advanced Science, and he has served on the program committees of VLDB, ICDE, and CIKM and co-chaired the ICDE 2023 Workshop on Big Data Analytics in Finance and Commerce.

Li Sun

Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications

Google Scholar ↗

Li Sun is an Associate Professor at School of Computer Science, BUPT, China. His research focuses on graph analysis and Riemannian geometry. He has authored over 80 papers in top venues including TPAMI, TOIS, TKDE, ICML, KDD, WWW and WSDM, and received an CIMK'22 best paper honorable mention. Dr. Sun has served as associate editor of Science Partner Journal of Intelligent Computing, guest editor for IEEE TKDE, area chairs of ICLR, ICML, KDD, senior program committee of AAAI, IJCAI, and hosted tutorials at KDD'26 on LLM personalization and ICDM'26 on graphs.

Yutong Ye

Zhejiang Sci-Tech University

Google Scholar ↗

Yutong Ye is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science and Technology at ZSTU, China. His research lies at the intersection of intelligent data management and AI infrastructure, with interests in AI-ready databases and graphs. His work has appeared in venues including SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, and KDD. He has served as a reviewer or program committee member for leading venues including VLDB, KDD, WWW, SIGIR and WSDM, and hosted KDD'26 tutorial on LLM personalization.

Zhiwei Liu

Recursive Inc.

Google Scholar ↗

Zhiwei Liu is a Member of Technical Staff at Recursive, working on agentic AI, evaluation for AI agents, information retrieval and large language models. Previously, he was a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft AI, and a Senior Research Scientist at Salesforce AI Research. He has authored over 100 papers in top venues including NeurIPS, ICLR, KDD, SIGIR, WWW and WSDM, served as guest editor for ACM TORS, and co-chair and organizer of Agent4IR workshops at KDD and SIGIR.

Leong Hou U

University of Macau

Google Scholar ↗

Leong Hou U is an Associate Professor, Assistant Dean, and Head of the Centre for Data Science at the University of Macau. His research bridges data management and machine learning, with interests in spatial and spatiotemporal databases, graph learning, reinforcement learning, and urban analytics. He has published more than 120 research papers, including leading venues such as SIGMOD, VLDB and ICDE. He has served as Program Co-chair of IEEE BigData 2025 and APWeb-WAIM 2021, and on program committees for SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, KDD, NeurIPS, ICML, and ICLR.

Xiaochun Yang

Northeastern University

Google Scholar ↗

Xiaochun Yang is a Professor and Dean at the School of Software Engineering, Northeastern University. Her research focuses on data management and analytics, including database theory and systems, text and time-series big data management, data quality management, data privacy protection, and intelligent recommendation. She has published extensively in leading database and AI venues, including IEEE TKDE, ACM TODS, SIGMOD, VLDB, and ICDE, and has served in professional roles in CCF database and collaborative computing committees, and general chair or co-chair of ICDM'26 and DASFAA'27.

Ruoming Jin

Kent State University

Google Scholar ↗

Ruoming Jin is a Professor of Computer Science at Kent State University. His research spans large language models, recommender systems, knowledge graphs, and privacy-preserving AI. He has published nearly 200 peer-reviewed papers and led or co-led more than $10 million in externally funded research from agencies including NSF and NIH. He is an NSF CAREER Award recipient, has received multiple best paper and distinguished paper recognitions, and has been listed among the world's top 2% scientists. He also co-founded TigerGraph, where he served as founding CTO, and is the founder and CEO of OppyAI.

Charu Aggarwal

IBM

Google Scholar ↗

Charu C. Aggarwal is a Distinguished Research Staff Member at the IBM T. J. Watson Research Center and an IBM Master Inventor. His research focuses on data mining and machine learning. He has received major honors including the ACM SIGKDD Innovation Award, IEEE Computer Society W. Wallace McDowell Award and IEEE ICDM Research Contributions Award. He currently serves as Editor-in-Chief of ACM TKDD and ACM Books, and has served as the general or program co-chair of the IEEE Big Data Conference'14, ICDM'15, CIKM'15, and KDD'16.

Philip S. Yu

University of Illinois Chicago

Google Scholar ↗

Philip S. Yu is a Distinguished Professor of Computer Science and the Wexler Chair in Information Technology at UIC. Before joining UIC, he was with the IBM T. J. Watson Research Center. His research interests include data mining, database systems, graph analysis, and Web applications. He has received major honors including the ACM SIGKDD Innovation Award, IEEE Computer Society Technical Achievement Award, IEEE Region 1 Award, and IEEE ICDM Research Contributions Award. He was the Editor-in-Chief of ACM TKDD and IEEE TKDE, and has served as program chairs or co-chairs of DASFAA'25, ICDE'95, and general chairs or co-chairs of CIKM'06, ICDM'02, ICDE'99, etc.
